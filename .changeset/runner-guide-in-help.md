The runner guide now ships inside the CLI. Each `qawolf runner` command's `--help` carries what used to be its section of `references/runner.md`, `qawolf help ref runner` prints the whole guide for the installed version, and `references/runner.md` is generated from it. The guide also covers mobile `runner actions` sequences: on mobile, send `tap` and `swipe`, never `click` or `drag`.
